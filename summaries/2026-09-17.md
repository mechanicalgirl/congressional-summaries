# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-09-17)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-145/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-145/daily-digest)

The Senate convened on September 15, 2026, to continue consideration of S. 4668, the Protect College Sports Act, successfully invoking cloture on the motion to proceed with a vote of 74-24. The chamber confirmed Matthew R. Byrne of Ohio as United States District Judge for the Southern District of Ohio by a vote of 52-45 and rejected a motion to invoke cloture on H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, by a vote of 49-50. The Senate introduced nine bills and three resolutions while processing routine appointments and other legislative business.

The House of Representatives passed multiple bills under suspension of rules addressing elder financial fraud, regulatory modernization, whistleblower protections, and various energy and environmental initiatives. The chamber tabled an impeachment resolution against former President Donald Trump by a vote of 232-147 and passed a resolution directing the President to remove armed forces from hostilities with Iran by a vote of 220-204. Multiple committees held hearings and markups on legislation spanning federal aviation administration oversight, nuclear spent fuel policy, and international financial systems.

The Senate processed numerous military officer appointments and promotions across all service branches, including the Air Force, Army, Navy, Space Force, and Coast Guard at various grades. Additionally, the State Department submitted multiple notifications of proposed arms sales to allied nations, including significant transactions with Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Lebanon, and Bahrain involving military aircraft, weapons systems, and defense equipment valued at billions of dollars collectively. These military assistance packages were designed to enhance security capabilities and interoperability among U.S. allies while supporting national security objectives in strategically important regions. Notable sales included Saudi Arabia's proposed acquisition of up to 20,000 Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System guidance sections valued at $1.96 billion and Kuwait's purchase of C-17 aircraft sustainment services for $484 million.
