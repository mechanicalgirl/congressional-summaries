# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-17)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-173/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-173/daily-digest)

On Monday, November 16, 2026, the Senate confirmed Daniel Desmond Domenico of Colorado as United States Circuit Judge for the Tenth Circuit by a vote of 51-46 and passed several bills including measures related to workers' compensation for federal employees, land trusts for Native American tribes, and child sexual abuse material storage. Four new public laws were signed on November 10 addressing IRS taxpayer advocate authority, Medicare wheelchair payments, national historic landmarks, and bankruptcy eligibility requirements. The Senate and House reconvened on Tuesday, November 17, with the Senate resuming debate on H.R. 2347 and scheduling a vote on Anna St. John's confirmation to be United States Circuit Judge for the Fifth Circuit at 2:15 p.m., while extensive committee hearings and business meetings were scheduled throughout the week covering nominations for executive and judicial positions as well as substantive matters including farm bill provisions, infrastructure projects, healthcare initiatives, and federal agency oversight.

Multiple Senate amendments submitted during this period addressed diverse policy areas, with most proposed as changes to H.R. 2347. Amendment SA 7011 would comprehensively restructure the Clean Water Act permitting provisions by establishing detailed procedures for state water quality certifications with specific timelines and frameworks for judicial review, creating a one-year maximum review period for applications with automatic waivers if agencies fail to act. The amendment also modifies Section 404 permitting by extending general permit terms from five to ten years and establishing a 150-day statute of limitations for judicial challenges to permits, while creating special expedited procedures for interstate transmission line projects with nine-month review periods.

Additional amendments targeted specialized sectors and infrastructure development, including SA 7012 by Mr. Ossoff and Mr. Warnock establishing accelerated permitting for solar projects with domestic content certification and reducing environmental review timelines by fifty percent. Amendments SA 7016 through SA 7018 addressed wholesale market access for behind-the-meter energy systems, community benefits from transmission funding, and shared utility rewards for grid efficiency improvements. Amendment SA 7015 revised tribal historic preservation program requirements and restructured consultation procedures for undertakings affecting historic properties, while SA 7021 would prohibit new data center construction on military installations by non-federal entities except for Department of Defense national security purposes.
