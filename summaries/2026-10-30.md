# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-10-30)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-165/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-165/daily-digest)

On Thursday, October 29, 2026, both chambers of Congress held abbreviated sessions, with the Senate meeting in pro forma session for 38 seconds and the House convening for only two minutes. During this minimal business session, the House introduced 34 public bills and 5 resolutions, ranging from H.R. 10918 to H.R. 10951, and filed 14 committee reports addressing topics such as amendments to the Clean Air Act, public health service authorizations, and natural resource management legislation. The chamber also received executive communications and prepared various bills for presidential consideration.

The Extensions of Remarks section of the session was devoted primarily to tributes honoring distinguished public servants, veterans, and community leaders from across the nation. Notable honorees included Mayor Marilyn Ezzy Ashcraft of Alameda for her climate leadership and inclusive governance, former Virginia State Senator Janet Howell for 32 years of legislative service focused on education and family protections, and Staff Sergeant Daniel Palenski, a World War II veteran celebrating his 100th birthday. Additional recognition was given to Thomas F. Balistreri, a Milwaukee educator and military veteran whose career influenced generations of students, and retired Kentucky State Senator Jimmy Higdon after 24 years of service, along with numerous recipients of community service awards acknowledging their contributions to veterans' affairs, disability rights, emergency services, and local governance.
