# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-09-12)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-143/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-143/daily-digest)

On September 10, 2026, the House of Representatives briefly convened to commemorate the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks by passing House Resolution 1520, which expressed solemn remembrance for the nearly 3,000 lives lost, honored first responders and military personnel, and reaffirmed the nation's commitment to combating terrorism. The chamber then introduced 25 public bills and 10 resolutions addressing diverse policy areas including small business support, healthcare fraud prevention, dietary supplement regulations, and elder protection from wire fraud, while committees reported seven bills covering topics such as Government Publishing Office authorities, National Park System leases, federal land transfers in Nevada, and a Customs and Border Protection program to adopt shelter dogs as support animals.

The Senate met in pro forma session on September 10 and is scheduled to reconvene on September 14, when it will vote on cloture for Matthew R. Byrne's nomination to serve as United States District Judge for the Southern District of Ohio. The Congressional Record also includes detailed expenditure reports documenting official foreign travel by various congressional committees and members during 2025 and 2026, itemizing costs for transportation, per diem, and other expenses incurred during delegations to countries across multiple continents including Argentina, Chile, South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Italy, Germany, and Australia. These travel reports, with committee totals ranging from approximately $9,000 to over $648,000 depending on delegation size and duration, comply with Public Law 95-384 requirements for transparency in congressional spending.
