# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-18)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-174/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-174/daily-digest)

The Senate convened on November 17, 2026, to continue consideration of H.R. 2347, the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act, which serves as a vehicle for the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act. The chamber established procedural timelines for amendments and votes while confirming Circuit Judge nominee Anna St. John of Louisiana by a vote of 52-46. The Senate also passed several bills including measures related to Gateway Arch National Park and Federal land transfers in California. Meanwhile, the House suspended rules to pass multiple measures concerning Indian land management, wildlife management, and water feasibility studies, while the Rules Committee advanced the Direct Seller and Real Estate Agent Harmonization Act and the American Franchise Act for consideration.

The Congressional Record includes an Extensions of Remarks section featuring personal tributes honoring individuals for their service and achievements. These tributes celebrated decorated Navy pilot Commander Leonard Merriman III, spiritual leaders Fleceia Comeaux and Pastor Chanda Crutcher recognized as 2026 She Leads America Christian Women of Distinction, and various community leaders. The section also memorialized notable figures including State Representative Fred Durhal Jr. and legendary civil rights activist Dolores Huerta, cofounder of the United Farm Workers.

The House advanced substantial legislation on natural resources and land transfers, including the Pit River Land Transfer Act returning approximately 557 acres to the Pit River Tribe in California and the Barona Group land transfer providing 847 acres to the Barona Band of Mission Indians. Additional measures authorized water infrastructure studies in South Dakota, transferred forest management in Arkansas, and reauthorized the African American Civil Rights Network and Earth Mapping Resources Initiative.
