# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-14)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-172/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-172/daily-digest)

The Senate will reconvene on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 3 p.m. to resume consideration of Daniel Desmond Domenico's nomination as United States Circuit Judge for the Tenth Circuit, with a confirmation vote scheduled for 5:30 p.m. Following that vote, the Senate will vote on a motion to invoke cloture on the Thune amendment regarding H.R. 2347, the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act, which serves as the legislative vehicle for the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act. The House of Representatives will reconvene on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business. During the House's brief session on Friday, November 13, four public bills were introduced, and numerous executive communications were received and referred to various committees, including final rules from the Department of Education, Securities and Exchange Commission, and Federal Aviation Administration. The House also saw discharge petition activity, with Representative Crow's petition on House Resolution 1596 gathering substantial support from 103 members on topics related to workforce and community initiatives.
