# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-26)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-178/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-178/daily-digest)

On November 24, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions before adjourning until Friday, November 27. The House introduced 23 new public bills addressing topics including congressional stock trading restrictions, voter identification requirements, agricultural trade partnerships, and Medicaid expansion for children. Additionally, the House Financial Services Committee reported seven bills dealing with payment scams, consumer financial protection, eviction assistance, and cryptocurrency management, while the ethics committee issued four reports regarding allegations against various representatives.

The Senate conducted only a brief pro forma session with no legislative action taken. Congressional committees received numerous executive communications from federal agencies, including final rules from the Federal Aviation Administration concerning airspace modifications and airworthiness directives. Expenditure reports documented official foreign travel expenses for House committees during the third and fourth quarters of 2026, with the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence reporting over $632,000 in total foreign travel expenditures for delegations visiting Asia, Europe, Africa, and other regions.
