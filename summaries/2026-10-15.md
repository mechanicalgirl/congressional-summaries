[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-160/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-160/daily-digest)

Congress held brief pro forma sessions on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, with both the Senate and House convening for minimal time before adjourning, with no substantive legislative work occurring. The Senate met for less than a minute while the House convened for only three minutes, and neither chamber held committee meetings or took votes during these abbreviated sessions. Both chambers are scheduled to reconvene with pro forma sessions on Thursday, October 15, with the Senate meeting at 9:30 a.m. and the House at 11 a.m. During the Tuesday session, the House introduced 15 public bills and one resolution, with H.R. 10238 regarding Small Business Administration cybersecurity resources receiving a committee report. Congressional members also used the Extensions of Remarks section to honor constituents, veterans, community organizations, and individuals for their service and contributions to their communities.
