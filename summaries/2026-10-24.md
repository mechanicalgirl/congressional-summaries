# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-10-24)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-163/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-163/daily-digest)

On October 22, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief sessions with minimal legislative activity. The Senate convened in pro forma session at 9:00 a.m. and adjourned until October 26, while the House met for just two minutes at 3:00 p.m. before adjourning. No committee meetings or hearings were scheduled in either chamber, and there were no recorded votes or quorum calls in the House.

The Congressional Record for this date primarily consists of Extensions of Remarks, featuring numerous tributes and recognitions from House members honoring constituents, veterans, community leaders, and organizations in their districts. These recognitions include tributes to military veterans and their families, commemoration of service anniversaries and milestones, and honors for individuals who made significant contributions to their communities in fields ranging from education and healthcare to civil rights and business development.

The House received fifty-three new bills and resolutions spanning diverse policy areas such as healthcare, veterans affairs, environmental protection, transportation safety, and election integrity, each accompanied by constitutional authority statements citing Article I of the Constitution. Additionally, the House received multiple executive communications from federal agencies including the Treasury Department, Department of Energy, Environmental Protection Agency, and Drug Enforcement Administration, transmitting final rules and proposed regulations related to stablecoin certification, Title IX regulations, controlled substance scheduling, air quality standards, and explosives storage. The Transportation and Infrastructure Committee reported H.R. 1382 regarding San Francisco Bay restoration with an amendment, after which both chambers adjourned to reconvene on October 26, 2026.
