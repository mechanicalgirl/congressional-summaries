# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-19)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-174/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-174/daily-digest)

The Senate convened on November 17, 2026, to continue consideration of H.R. 2347, the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act, while confirming Anna St. John of Louisiana as United States Circuit Judge for the Fifth Circuit by a vote of 52-46. The chamber passed several measures including H.R. 5254 authorizing the Gateway Arch Park Foundation to host private events, H.R. 8454 transferring administrative jurisdiction over federal land in California, and S. Res. 959 recognizing the death of former Senator Jay Rockefeller IV. The Senate also discharged resolutions designating October 12, 2026, as National Loggers Day and the week of September 21-25, 2026, as National Hazing Awareness Week, while reaching a unanimous consent agreement to continue consideration of H.R. 2347 on Wednesday morning. The House met briefly to pass seven measures under suspension of rules, including legislation assisting tribal governments in buffalo management, requiring regional management frameworks for cormorant control, transferring California land to the Barona Band of Mission Indians, and approving a commemorative work for Thomas Paine.

The Congressional Record included multiple tributes honoring distinguished individuals from across the country. Representative Troy A. Carter celebrated the life of United States Navy Commander Leonard Merriman III, a decorated naval officer and commercial airline pilot who mentored young people interested in military service. Representative Lisa C. McClain recognized two recipients of the 2026 She Leads America Christian Woman of Distinction awards: Flecelia Comeaux, who has spent 27 years with the Fellowship of Christian Athletes, and Pastor Chanda Crutcher, whose leadership strengthened communities through faith-based initiatives. Representative James R. Walkinshaw honored recipients of the 2026 SPARC Hero Awards, recognizing efforts to support adults with severe and multiple disabilities in Northern Virginia.

The House passed two significant wildlife management bills on a bipartisan basis. The Don Young Doug LaMalfa Indian Buffalo Management Act directs the Department of the Interior to work with Indian Tribes to strengthen their capacity to restore, manage, and conserve buffalo and habitat through grants and technical assistance, recognizing buffalo's cultural and spiritual significance to Indigenous communities while respecting tribal sovereignty and treaty rights.
