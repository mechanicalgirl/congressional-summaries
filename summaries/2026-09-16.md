# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-09-16)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-144/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-144/daily-digest)

Congress convened on September 14, 2026, with the Senate confirming Matthew R. Byrne as United States District Judge for the Southern District of Ohio while scheduling consideration of other judicial nominations. The Senate received numerous new nominations for federal judges, ambassadors, senior executive positions, and military officers, and reported multiple bills from committee addressing aviation safety, communications equipment security, cybersecurity of commercial satellites, and resource management. The House passed various measures under suspension of the rules concerning wildlife management, national parks, Native American affairs, and maritime issues, while also considering resolutions regarding Environmental Protection Agency rules and sanctions on Russia and Iran.

The Extensions of Remarks section featured congressional tributes to individuals and institutions, including Representative Cleo Fields honoring Living Word Church in Baton Rouge and Representative Christian D. Menefee recognizing Prairie View A&M University on its 150th anniversary. Additional tributes recognized Richard Phillip Stroud Sr.'s 100th birthday and his distinguished naval and aeronautical engineering career, Louisiana East First Jurisdiction's over a century of religious service, the Light the World Giving Machine charitable initiative that raised over $600,000 in 2024, and Interamerican Bank's fifty years of service to South Florida.

The House considered three significant financial services bills addressing modern regulatory challenges. The FUTURES Act requires federal banking regulators and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau to assess their technological systems and submit comprehensive reports to Congress every five years, addressing concerns about outdated technologies affecting their ability to detect illegal activities and emerging risks. The GUARD Act permits state, local, and tribal law enforcement agencies to use federal grant funds to investigate elder financial fraud and pig butchering schemes, with support for training on blockchain intelligence tools, recognizing that Americans lost an estimated $200 billion to fraud schemes in recent years. The Northern Nevada Economic Development and Conservation Act authorizes land conveyances and sales totaling approximately 150,000 acres, with proceeds distributed to education funds, county budgets, and conservation accounts, while designating nearly 150,000 acres as new wilderness areas and transferring 2,423 acres to the Washoe Tribe as trust land, enjoying broad bipartisan support despite some Democratic concerns about federal land disposals and environmental protections.
