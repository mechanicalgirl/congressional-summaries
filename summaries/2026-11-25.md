# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-25)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-178/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-178/daily-digest)

On November 24, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions before adjourning until November 27, with the Senate meeting for 36 seconds and the House convening for 3 minutes. During these sessions, five enrolled bills were signed into law, including measures transferring federal land to Native American tribes in California and authorizing private events at Gateway Arch National Park. The House introduced 23 new public bills and filed reports on seven bills from the Financial Services Committee that addressed payment scams, consumer protection, eviction assistance, and mortgage insurance provisions. Additionally, the Ethics Committee filed four reports concerning allegations against Representatives Jimmy Gomez, Troy Nehls, and Mike Collins. Both chambers are scheduled to reconvene in pro forma session on Friday, November 27 at noon, with no committee meetings planned for that date.
