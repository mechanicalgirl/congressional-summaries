# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-10-03)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-156/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-156/daily-digest)

On Thursday, October 1, 2026, the Senate held a brief pro forma session while the House met for only three minutes to introduce 80 public bills, 1 private bill, and 17 resolutions. The Senate Banking Committee completed hearings on four nominations, including candidates for Assistant Secretary of the Treasury and Assistant Secretary of Housing and Urban Development. Both chambers are scheduled to reconvene in pro forma sessions on Monday, October 5 at 4 p.m. and 4:30 p.m. respectively.

The Extensions of Remarks section featured numerous tributes honoring retiring law enforcement officers, community leaders, and organizational milestones, along with recognitions of awareness observances including Ostomy Awareness Day and International Plasma Awareness Week. Members introduced bills and resolutions addressing varied legislative priorities such as Great Lakes conservation and national health awareness campaigns.

The House introduced over 100 public bills and resolutions spanning healthcare, immigration, veterans affairs, education, and infrastructure. Notable legislation included measures to establish an Urban Health Transformation Program, restrict arbitration clauses in retirement plans, impose sanctions on West Bank and Gaza settlements, enhance H-1B visa penalties, and strengthen Medicaid fraud prevention. Additional bills addressed police training standards, campus sexual assault prevention, disability rights for political candidates, voting accessibility, artificial intelligence safety for minors, automatic license plate reader restrictions, and lithium-ion battery transportation regulations.

The House approved three Senate-enrolled bills covering amendments to the Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Act, seafood country-of-origin standards, and ERISA stock valuation provisions. The chamber also received executive communications from federal agencies regarding whistleblower awards, deposit insurance, arms export controls, and regulatory updates from the Departments of State, Labor, Transportation, and Health and Human Services before adjourning until October 5, 2026.
