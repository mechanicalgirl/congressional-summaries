# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-03)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-166/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-166/daily-digest)

On November 2, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions lasting only a few minutes, with the Senate convening for less than one minute and the House for two minutes. Both chambers are scheduled to reconvene on Thursday, November 5, for additional pro forma sessions. During the House session on November 2, two public bills and three resolutions were introduced, including legislation focused on forest health and wildfire risk reduction, and a program establishing state ombudsmen for individuals with developmental disabilities. Two committee reports were also filed addressing independent contractor classification and worker protections under the Fair Labor Standards Act. The Congressional Record from this session includes numerous Extensions of Remarks honoring various individuals and organizations, including veterans receiving Congressional Veteran Commendations, educators, business owners, and organizations celebrating significant milestones such as the American Hiking Society's 50th anniversary and the Quarryville Lions Club's 100 years of service.
