[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-135/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-135/daily-digest)

On August 24, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions lasting only a few minutes, with the Senate convening for 33 seconds and the House for 3 minutes. No votes were taken or committee meetings held during these minimal sessions. Both chambers are scheduled to reconvene on Thursday, August 27, with the Senate meeting at 8 a.m. and the House at 9 a.m., also in pro forma sessions.

During this legislative period, the House introduced 11 public bills numbered H.R. 10138-10148, along with one private bill for relief of Maria Cordova and four resolutions. The resolutions addressed varied topics including sanctions on Chinese synthetic narcotics, data center disclosure requirements, sunscreen availability in national parks, and recognition of the Artemis II lunar mission crew. Additionally, the Congressional Record included several tributes to notable individuals and organizations, honoring Reverend Ronald G.
