# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-10-28)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-164/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-164/daily-digest)

On Monday, October 26, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions with minimal legislative activity. The Senate convened at 5:30 p.m. under Acting President pro tempore Cynthia M. Lummis and immediately adjourned until Thursday, October 29 at 2 p.m., while the House met at 12:30 p.m. under Speaker pro tempore H. Morgan Griffith and adjourned two minutes later until Thursday at noon.

The House introduced 11 public bills and 3 resolutions during the session, addressing a range of policy areas including Universal Savings Accounts, healthcare transparency, death in custody reporting amendments, animal welfare protections, swine health improvements, arms export controls for Ukraine, cybersecurity standards for cloud infrastructure, and forest restoration initiatives. Beyond legislative introductions, the House entered numerous tributes and recognitions into the record honoring various individuals and organizations. These included tributes to reggae artist Beres Hammond, World War II veteran Stuart Gordon, and the Roots and Routes Indo-US Chamber of Commerce and its 30 Under 30 honorees, as well as recognitions of retired community servant Cheryl Meadows and various local institutions celebrating anniversaries. Representative Marcy Kaptur also had remarks regarding accountability related to Jeffrey Epstein and Leslie Wexner entered into the record, while colleagues commended community organizations including KidsMatter, the Arcada Theatre, and Pluckemin Presbyterian Church for their contributions and milestones.
