# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-08-28)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-136/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-136/daily-digest)

On Thursday, August 27, 2026, both chambers of Congress conducted abbreviated sessions in preparation for an extended recess. The Senate met in pro forma session for approximately thirty seconds before adjourning until Monday, August 31, while the House held a similarly brief session lasting just over three minutes, with no votes taken in either chamber.

Before adjourning, the House introduced 34 public bills and 8 resolutions, and numerous committee reports were filed addressing diverse legislative matters including tax-exempt organization disclosure requirements, North Atlantic right whale protections, Indian Health Service reorganization, and various natural resource management issues. Representatives also used the Extensions of Remarks section to honor constituents and notable figures, including recognition of a Tuskegee Airman's legacy and commendations for individuals in law enforcement, education, and entrepreneurship.

The Senate is scheduled to reconvene on August 31 at 11:30 a.m. for another pro forma session, while the House will meet at noon on the same day with its program to be announced.
