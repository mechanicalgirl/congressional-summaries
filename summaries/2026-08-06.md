# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-08-06)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-127/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-127/daily-digest)

The Senate on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, continued consideration of H.R. 6500, the AGOA Extension Act, which functions as a legislative vehicle for a continuing resolution. During this session, several bills related to health, Indian affairs, and infrastructure were passed, while the Senate agreed to a resolution recognizing the 80th anniversary of the Fulbright Program. Four proposed amendments to H.R. 6500 were received, with senators offering modifications on topics including federal award policies, agricultural regulations, and surface transportation extensions. Committee activity focused on hearings regarding Medicaid, biotechnology in healthcare, AI surveillance pricing, and nominations for various federal positions.

The Senate reconvened on Wednesday, August 5 at 10:30 a.m. to continue post-cloture consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 6500. A scheduled cloture vote on the nomination of Erica Schwartz for Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention occurred at 11:30 a.m., with a confirmation vote set for 2:15 p.m. if cloture was invoked. The Senate also planned to consider en bloc nominations and a motion regarding the PFAS reporting rule under the Toxic Substances Control Act if Senator Whitehouse proceeded with such motions.

The Senate passed two significant healthcare bills addressing serious neurological conditions. The National Plan for Epilepsy Act requires the Secretary of Health and Human Services to review federal epilepsy programs and research to identify knowledge gaps and improve health outcomes, with findings due to Congress within two years. The Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Reauthorization Act of 2026 extends authorization for ALS research grant programs through fiscal year 2031, enhances oversight of expanded access grants through safety data reporting requirements, expands FDA action plan requirements for neurodegenerative diseases, and mandates program effectiveness reporting.
