# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-08-05)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-126/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-126/daily-digest)

During the week of August 4-7, 2026, the Senate will continue post-cloture consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 6500, the AGOA Extension Act serving as a legislative vehicle for a continuing resolution, with roll call votes expected on Tuesday and a recess scheduled from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for party conferences. Multiple Senate committees are scheduled to meet during this period, including hearings on Medicaid, biotechnology in healthcare, Social Security solvency, pending nominations, and artificial intelligence safety for children, along with business meetings to consider bills ranging from postal facility designations to federal employee programs and tribal trust lands. The Senate has also set a cloture motion on the nomination of Erica Schwartz to be Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, with the vote occurring following disposition of the H.R. 6500 vehicle, and begun consideration of en bloc nominations scheduled for similar treatment.

The Senate passed three bills aimed at addressing public health and financial challenges, including legislation to ban high-concentration sodium nitrite products to protect youth from poisoning, amendments to bankruptcy eligibility requirements that raise debt limits for small business and consumer debtors, and the Foreign Robocall Elimination Act, which establishes a federal taskforce to combat unlawful international robocalls and strengthens enforcement mechanisms. Senator Schumer introduced the "No Payoffs for Pardons Act," which seeks to regulate presidential clemency by requiring disclosure of financial benefits provided to pardon recipients and expanding federal bribery statutes to include executive clemency as a form of corruption. The Senate also advanced a continuing resolution with bipartisan support to prevent a shutdown while negotiations on full appropriations bills continue into the fall, along with processing nominations of federal officials and various amendments related to sanctions policy and international affairs.
