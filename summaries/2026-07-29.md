# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-07-29)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-122/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-122/daily-digest)

The Senate advanced Walter Clayton's nomination as Director of National Intelligence on Monday, July 27, 2026, invoking cloture by a 51-43 vote with final confirmation scheduled for Tuesday, July 28. The chamber also reported multiple bills from committees addressing biosimilar drug requirements, human rights concerns in Xinjiang, hospital price transparency, and various foreign policy matters including sanctions on Syria, support for Taiwan, Arctic security, and Sudan stability. Eight new bills were introduced covering topics ranging from tax code modifications to mental health licensure portability and quantum technology development. The House convened briefly on Monday, introducing 30 public bills and 6 resolutions before adjourning within two minutes, with no recorded votes taken, and will reconvene in pro forma session on Thursday, July 30.

The Senate received numerous executive communications from federal agencies on July 22, 2026, focusing on environmental and regulatory matters referred to the Committee on Environment and Public Works, including EPA reports on California nonroad engine pollution standards, ocean-going vessel emissions, and various chemical regulations. Additional communications concerning next-generation 911 services, counter-unmanned aircraft systems for law enforcement, and Department of Transportation regulations on motor carrier safety and electronic logging devices were directed to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation. The Senate also processed reports on baby crib safety standards, shellfish definitions, summer flounder fishing quotas, and Federal Aviation Administration airspace designations near Texas. House Bill 4541, which would reauthorize the Young Women's Breast Health Education and Awareness Requires Learning Young Act of 2009, was referred to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions by unanimous consent.
