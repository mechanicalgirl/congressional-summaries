# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-28)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-179/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-179/daily-digest)

On Friday, November 27, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions before adjourning until Monday, November 30. The House introduced one bill regarding egg product inspection and one resolution commending the Kansas City Current's 2026 National Women's Soccer League Championship, while the Senate took no substantive action. Representative Kevin Hern of Oklahoma resigned from his House seat to pursue the U.S. Senate seat vacated by former Senator Markwayne Mullin. The House also enrolled and signed two bills: one establishing consumer standards for lithium-ion batteries and another amending the Investment Company Act of 1940 regarding securities redemption protections.

Additionally, the Speaker appointed four individuals to the National Advisory Committee on Institutional Quality and Integrity, with three recommended by the Majority Leader and one by the Minority Leader. The House Committee on Rules scheduled a hearing for Monday, November 30 to review four bills covering topics including bipartisan job creation and affordability, insider trading restrictions, real estate and direct seller regulations, and franchise business standards. The Congressional Record also included tributes to retired Colonel James Charles Jackson, a Tuskegee University alumnus and former Aflac executive who passed away at age 97, and to South Carolina State Senator Billy Garrett upon his retirement.
