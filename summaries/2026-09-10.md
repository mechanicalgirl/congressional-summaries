# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-09-10)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-142/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-142/daily-digest)

Both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, with the Senate convening for less than a minute and the House meeting for only three minutes before adjourning. The Senate will reconvene at 3 p.m. on Thursday, September 10, while the House will return at 3:30 p.m. the same day, both in pro forma sessions. During the abbreviated House session, 22 public bills and 6 resolutions were introduced, with 13 committee reports filed on various matters ranging from veterans' benefits and tax administration to education policy and civil rights transparency. Several House members submitted personal explanations for votes they missed due to their absence from Washington, while others used the Extensions of Remarks to honor local heroes, deceased public servants, and community institutions in their districts, including firefighters, law enforcement officers, church congregations, and recipients of service awards.
