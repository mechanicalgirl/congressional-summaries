# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-11-04)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-166/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-166/daily-digest)

On Monday, November 2, 2026, both chambers of Congress held brief pro forma sessions lasting only minutes, with the Senate meeting for less than one minute and the House convening for just two minutes before adjourning. Both chambers scheduled their next sessions for Thursday, November 5, and neither chamber held committee meetings or votes during this period. The House introduced two public bills and three resolutions, including measures on forest health and wildfire management, state ombudsman programs for individuals with developmental disabilities, and a constitutional amendment proposal regarding campaign finance regulation. The House Committee on Education and Workforce filed reports on two bills addressing the classification of locum tenens professionals as independent contractors and worker access to employer equity. Multiple executive communications from the Department of Defense were also referred to relevant committees, primarily concerning military personnel management, acquisition procedures, and defense capabilities.
