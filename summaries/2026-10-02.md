# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-10-02)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-155/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-155/daily-digest)

On September 30, 2026, the Senate passed numerous bills and confirmed multiple nominees while conducting routine business before an extended recess. Legislative accomplishments included measures addressing national parks, water resources, tax administration, cybersecurity in healthcare, emergency alert systems, and protections for wrongfully detained Americans. The Senate confirmed Keith Sonderling as Secretary of Labor, multiple ambassadors, and various military nominees, while also unanimously approving several resolutions designating observances such as National Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Day and Sickle Cell Disease Awareness Month.

The Senate established a schedule of pro forma sessions through November 9, 2026, when substantive business will resume with consideration of the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act. Senate Democrats, led by Minority Leader Schumer, used their closing remarks to criticize Trump administration policies on inflation, healthcare access, and election integrity, while claiming credit for blocking or mitigating several Republican initiatives. Republicans maintained control of legislative priorities as both parties prepared for the upcoming election season and potential shifts in congressional dynamics.

Senate Amendment 6847, the Taxpayer Assistance and Service Act, represents a comprehensive ten-title measure modernizing IRS operations and improving taxpayer services. The amendment addresses tax administration through digitization of returns and correspondence, real-time dashboards tracking IRS performance, expanded electronic access to taxpayer information, and extended callback services. It streamlines offers-in-compromise procedures, eliminates installment agreement fees for low-income taxpayers, and creates programs to identify struggling taxpayers and inform them of collection alternatives while substantially expanding judicial review authorities and strengthening enforcement mechanisms against tax return preparers.

Senator Markey's amendment to H.R. 9340 establishes comprehensive federal standards for data center interconnection and renewable energy requirements, mandating that data centers interconnected at the transmission level derive 100 percent of their peak megawatt demand from new renewable energy sources. Senator Gallego's separate amendment imposes a one percent tax on direct freshwater consumption by artificial intelligence data centers. These provisions address growing concerns about data center infrastructure costs and environmental impacts related to AI development.
