# Congressional Summary (run on 2026-07-30)

[https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-123/daily-digest](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-172/issue-123/daily-digest)

The Senate convened on July 28, 2026, to confirm Walter Clayton of New York as Director of National Intelligence by a vote of 51-47 and to advance consideration of the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act. The session was marked by tributes to the late Senator Lindsey Graham, whose funeral service was held at the National Cathedral that morning, with colleagues recognizing the Russia sanctions legislation as his most significant legislative achievement. Clayton's nomination proceeded amid considerable controversy, with supporters highlighting his experience as U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York prosecuting national security threats, while opponents including Senator Ron Wyden raised serious concerns about his promotion of election conspiracy theories, refusal to acknowledge the 2020 election results, and involvement in disputed subpoenas issued to New York Times journalists.

Beyond the nomination, the Senate voted 86-12 to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 5334, the Supporting Early-Childhood Educators' Deductions Act, which was set for continued consideration on July 29. Senators addressed substantive policy concerns including fraud in state Medicaid programs and voter registration issues, with Senator Marsha Blackburn highlighting billions in questionable expenditures in California and Minnesota and noncitizen voter registration problems in states like New Jersey. Senator Peter Welch raised concerns about the revised sanctions bill's language regarding countries "facilitating" sanctions evasion, arguing it constituted an overly broad delegation of tariff authority to the executive branch. The chamber also reported seven health-related bills from committee and discharged two joint resolutions challenging federal agency rules through the Congressional Review Act process.
