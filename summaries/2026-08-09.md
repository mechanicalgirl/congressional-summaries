On Friday, August 7, 2026, the Senate passed several significant pieces of legislation and confirmed numerous executive nominees. The chamber passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act by a vote of 86-11, along with the Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act by 90-6. Todd Blanche of Florida was confirmed as Attorney General by a narrow 50-49 vote. The Senate also passed multiple other bills and resolutions, including the Stop Secret Spending Act, the Doug LaMalfa Federal Disaster Tax Relief Certainty Act, and designated August 7 as National Lighthouse Day.

The Senate confirmed 51 executive nominees en bloc by a 51-47 vote, including ambassadors, cabinet officers, and federal judges across multiple agencies. The Senate will reconvene in pro forma session on Monday, August 10, 2026, with several significant measures pending consideration, including motions on the Help America Vote Act and Digital Asset Market Clarity Act scheduled for votes in mid-September.

Senate Amendment 6764 proposes comprehensive reforms to the Public Safety Officers' Benefit Program, establishing strict timelines for processing death benefit claims with requirements that the Bureau of Justice Assistance notify claimants of missing information within 90 days and issue final determinations within 270 days. The amendment expands the Bureau's authority to subpoena public agencies that fail to provide documentation, implements mandatory annual audits, and mandates congressional reporting on program backlogs and performance improvements.

The legislation establishes a Congressional Commission on the Future of College Athletics with 24 members tasked with studying compensation structures, athlete eligibility standards, and health and safety compliance. The commission must submit a preliminary report within 30 months and a final report within five years. The section authorizes recruitment restrictions during designated periods while protecting athlete transfer rights, establishes antitrust exemptions for specific athletics rules, and prohibits reduction of women's and Olympic sports opportunities at large institutions for nine years.

Senate Concurrent Resolution 38 establishes the congressional budget for fiscal year 2027 through 2036, projecting federal revenues of $4.48 trillion in 2027 growing to $6.34 trillion by 2036.
